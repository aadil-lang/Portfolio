## 2. Narrative Significance: From Single Chains to Cyclic Graphs & Global Solvers
- **The Problem with Linear Workflows**:
  In Projects 01 and 02, pipelines were fundamentally directed acyclic workflows (DAGs): input $\rightarrow$ prompt $\rightarrow$ parse $\rightarrow$ render. When generating standardized exam forms containing hundreds of items, linear generation cannot enforce global multi-variable balance.
- **The Concept of Cyclic Graph Orchestration**:
  Introducing LangGraph with cycles allows the system to self-heal: an item that fails review is routed back to a specific generator node with corrective feedback, rather than throwing away the entire context.
- **The Bridge to Deterministic Operations Research**:
  Introducing Integer Linear Programming (ILP) represents the insight that LLMs should never be asked to perform statistical stratification or resource allocation. The model generates individual candidate items; a deterministic mathematical optimization algorithm selects the exact subset that satisfies the curriculum distribution.
