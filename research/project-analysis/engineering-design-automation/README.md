## Executive Summary
Engineering Design Automation represents the ultimate realization of the portfolio's central thesis: **Deterministic Authority over Probabilistic Intelligence**. Grounded directly in the author's B.Tech in Mechanical Engineering, the system bridges natural-language engineering requirements with closed-form mathematical solvers, authoritative metallurgical databases, parametric 3D CAD modeling, and 2D manufacturing drawings.

The system enforces an uncompromising boundary:
- **AI / LLMs**: Handle linguistic ambiguity, requirement interpretation, and field-level text span extraction with confidence scoring.
- **Deterministic Engineering Kernels**: Sole authority for torsional stress calculations, von Mises distortion-energy shear yield conversion ($S_{sy} = 0.57735 \cdot S_y$), metric nominal diameter series quantization, 3D OpenCascade BRep geometry generation, and CAD-to-drawing dimensional cross-validation.
